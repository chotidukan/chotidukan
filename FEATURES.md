# Included features

- Saffron, ivory and deep-green responsive storefront.
- Optional location access, 1 km store-radius filtering, store-name search and a sample Faridabad neighbourhood.
- Product search understands common English, Hindi and transliterated equivalents, plus merchant-entered aliases.
- One basket and checkout across multiple stores, with individual delivery fees and estimated countdowns.
- Test-payment success and decline paths; no payment gateway or money transfer.
- Shopkeeper inventory, product creation/editing, store creation/settings and order handling.
- Optional best-before stock batches, earliest-date-first allocation, held past-date inventory, seven-day inventory filters and batch-specific near-date pricing.
- Daily, alternate-day or selected-weekday product subscriptions with one-hour IST delivery slots; edit/pause/resume/cancel and manually generate a demo occurrence.
- Supabase email/password accounts and persistent account-owned workspaces; PostgreSQL revision checks prevent lost updates.
- Bundled generated grocery artwork at `public/grocery-hero.png` and `public/product-atlas.png`.

Read README.md before deploying. This is a demo with sample data and simulated payments/deliveries.
